## Phase Goal

**As a** 作詞人 Andy（電腦、平板、手機都會用）, **I want to** 在三種裝置上都能完整查韻、寫詞、用 AI 建議，而且縮放不吃邊、大量字卡不卡、手機輸入不被鍵盤擋, **so that** 不管拿哪台裝置都能順手寫出押韻好詞。

(Derived from the ROADMAP Phase 5 goal line; see 05-01-PLAN.md.)

<objective>
Phone and tablet bottom sheets become a complete, symmetric three-flow UI (查韻 · 歌詞 · AI 建議) with obvious ways to close, no hidden focus traps, and sheet contents that stay usable with many sections or many words — proven by a real-click full-flow test on all five reference viewports.

Purpose: UI-01 on S/M ("查韻、寫詞、AI 建議三個主流程都能點到", CX-7) and the end-to-end flow check of CX-11.
Output: 3-tab bar, sheet handle, Esc, sheet sizing and internals, flow test.

Decisions (IDs as defined in 05-01-PLAN.md objective): D-06, D-17, CX-7, CX-11, OD-7. Claude's discretion (drawer details): the AI sheet pins its CTA at the bottom by ordering it after the word grid with CSS `order` on ≤ 900 (DOM unchanged) — `position: sticky; bottom: 0` only pins an element whose natural position is below the visible area, so with the button above the words (desktop order) UI-SPEC §5.4's "always reachable while scrolling words" would not hold.
</objective>
