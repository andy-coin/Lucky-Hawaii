## Phase Goal

**As a** 作詞人 Andy, **I want to** 用一個指令把多個授權相容的台灣詞庫自動清洗成同一格式的「詞庫包」，並在網站偏好設定裡切換比較, **so that** 查到的詞更符合台灣日常語感，簡體、中國用語與冷僻詞大幅減少.

<objective>
Add the other two licence-clean sources as independent packs — 小麥注音詞庫 (MIT + libtabe BSD) and 唯音台灣詞庫（精簡版） (MulanPSL-2.0 + libtabe BSD, without the 教育部 component) — so Andy can switch among four packs and the next plan's single-source (gray) rule has all three sources to compare.

Purpose: LEX-01 "several sources" and LEX-07 "each source an independent pack in one format".
Output: two new parsers, two new packs with NOTICE + licence texts, manifest with four packs, settings test against the real manifest.

This plan implements: A1 (McBopomofo and Vanguard non-MOE verdicts), A2 (NOTICE per pack), B1, B2, B3 (four packs listed), OD-5. Decision IDs: see 06-01-PLAN.md objective.
</objective>
