## Phase Goal

**As a** 作詞人 Andy, **I want to** 用一個指令把多個授權相容的台灣詞庫自動清洗成同一格式的「詞庫包」，並在網站偏好設定裡切換比較, **so that** 查到的詞更符合台灣日常語感，簡體、中國用語與冷僻詞大幅減少.

<objective>
Make pack switching safe to use for weeks of comparison: browser storage stays bounded (active pack + one), a new pack version invalidates only that pack's cache, corrections apply on every pack without duplicates, and the About dialog tells Andy where the active pack's words come from.

Purpose: LEX-06 (old caches invalidate automatically, nothing grows without bound) and A2 (About lists the active pack's sources) on top of the 06-02 loader.
Output: LRU registry and eviction, per-pack version tests, overrides de-dup, About 「詞庫來源」 block.

This plan implements: B4 (keep at most active + 1, never mix), B5, A2 (About), LEX-06. The `?v=` stays 0.96.8 (OD-2: already bumped in 06-02, nothing deployed since). Decision IDs: see 06-01-PLAN.md objective.
</objective>
