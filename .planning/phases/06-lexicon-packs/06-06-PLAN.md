## Phase Goal

**As a** 作詞人 Andy, **I want to** 用一個指令把多個授權相容的台灣詞庫自動清洗成同一格式的「詞庫包」，並在網站偏好設定裡切換比較, **so that** 查到的詞更符合台灣日常語感，簡體、中國用語與冷僻詞大幅減少.

<objective>
Give every pack a spot-checkable deletion report and a one-command rescue path, so Andy can see why words disappeared and bring a wrongly removed word back in a way that survives every future rebuild.

Purpose: LEX-05 (reason per deleted word, report with statistics and samples, rescue through the correction flow).
Output: lex/report.py, REPORT.md + stats.json per pack, `build.py rescue`, report consistency in verify, a site test proving a rescued word comes back.

This plan implements: D1 (machine list from 06-05 + human report here, kept outside data/), D2 (rescue via keep.json and overrides added), C4 (pending counts and token estimate shown). Decision IDs: see 06-01-PLAN.md objective.
</objective>
