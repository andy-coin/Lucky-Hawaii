## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story form — surfaced, not invented): 段落變成可拖曳排序、可調整高度的模組，搭配結構化標籤、段落顏色、字數統計與 Undo/Redo；AI 候選詞可以直接拖進歌詞裡。

<objective>
Let writers drag an AI word card (or a rhyme char card) straight into the lyrics at an exact caret position — mouse drag on desktop, ~400 ms long-press on touch — while plain clicks keep their old meaning and nothing is inserted by accident.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 5 (AI-06) and EDIT-03's "AI 詞插入" coverage.
Output: js/word-drag.js (pointer session, mirror caret, phone tab switch, dwell expand), styles, tests/word-drag.spec.mjs.

Decisions implemented: CONTEXT F1–F8, D2 (word insert), G1, G2; UI-SPEC D-08, D-13, D-14, §8. Recorded for UAT: F1 (char cards draggable too), F7 (phone auto-switch to 歌詞), the desktop collapsed-panel toast (RESEARCH OQ4).
</objective>
