## Phase Goal

Decisions implemented: CONTEXT A3 (label rewrite on change), B1, B2, B3, G1, G2; UI-SPEC D-01, D-02, D-13 (add), D-17. The custom-name rename moves to the settings popover in 09-08; until then a section keeps its Custom name but cannot be renamed (intermediate state inside this phase only).
</objective>
