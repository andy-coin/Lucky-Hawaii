## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story form — surfaced, not invented): 段落變成可拖曳排序、可調整高度的模組，搭配結構化標籤、段落顏色、字數統計與 Undo/Redo；AI 候選詞可以直接拖進歌詞裡。

<objective>
Right-click (desktop) or long-press (touch) a section name to open 「段落設定」: pick a preset or custom color, choose the type directly, name a Custom section, and move the section up / down — every change undoable and saved, colors shown as a left bar plus a light tint that keeps text readable in every theme.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 2, right-click color half (EDIT-06); restores custom naming (removed with the input label in 09-02); touch fallback for reordering (CONTEXT C3); EDIT-03 coverage of custom name and color.
Output: popover module in js/sections.js, color rendering, styles, contrast / fit / IME specs.

Decisions implemented: CONTEXT B4, B5, C3 (popover move), D2 (custom name, color), G1; UI-SPEC D-03, D-04, D-05, D-06, D-12, D-13, D-15, §3, §9.3.
</objective>
