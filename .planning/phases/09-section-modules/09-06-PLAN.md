## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story form — surfaced, not invented): 段落變成可拖曳排序、可調整高度的模組，搭配結構化標籤、段落顏色、字數統計與 Undo/Redo；AI 候選詞可以直接拖進歌詞裡。

<objective>
Replace the buggy header-wide HTML5 drag with a dedicated ⋮⋮ handle driven by Pointer Events, so sections can be reordered with a mouse, a finger or Alt+↑ / Alt+↓, with a ghost, a drop line, edge auto-scroll, Esc cancel, one undo step per move and screen-reader feedback.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 1, reorder half (EDIT-02), and EDIT-03's "排序" coverage.
Output: drag session and keyboard moves in js/sections.js, handle in the header, styles, tests/drag-lib.mjs (reused by 09-08 and 09-09), tests/sections-drag.spec.mjs, lyrics -darwin baselines.

Decisions implemented: CONTEXT C1, C2, C3 (keyboard; the popover's 上移 / 下移 arrive in 09-08 using lhSecMoveBy), D2 (reorder), G1, G2; UI-SPEC D-01, D-07, D-14, §4, §11 gesture exclusivity / mode switch / panel collapse.
</objective>
