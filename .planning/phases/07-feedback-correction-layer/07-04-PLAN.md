## Phase Goal

**As a** 作詞人（使用者）與維護者 Andy, **I want to** 用更少步驟回報有問題的字詞，並在後台把同一個詞的回報合併批改、一鍵套用到線上修正層、需要時一鍵撤回，累積後用一個腳本寫回 `data/` 再安全清空修正層, **so that** 修正很快就讓線上使用者重新整理就看到，而且不會重複套用或遺失.

<objective>
Tracer for the reporting half (FB-01): a visible 🚩 回報 button on both batch toolbars opens one-page report dialog; picking a reason and pressing 送出回報 posts one record per selected item. Then the dialog gets every state from UI-SPEC §3 (multi, cap, dedupe, failure, 429, accessibility) and the layout/theme proof across devices.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 1 (「回報入口明顯、幾步內就能送出；手機也有不靠長按的回報方式」) for character cards; AI word cards and long-press removal follow in 07-06.
Output: toolbar buttons, report dialog, postFeedbackRaw, session dedupe, styles and tokens, report-entry specs, refreshed darwin screenshot baselines.

Decisions implemented: A1 (toolbar half), A3, A4, E8, D-01, D-02, D-06, D-07, D-08, D-09, D-10, D-19, D-20 (site labels).
</objective>
