## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story form — surfaced, not invented): 歌詞改以資料模型為準，打字（含注音組字）不再被打斷；可以像 Google Docs 一樣用側邊分頁管理多首歌，並匯出、備份、換裝置還原。

<objective>
Two open tabs stay in sync without ever interrupting typing: a change saved in one tab reloads the other tab's screen with a toast, but only after its editor loses focus; edits to different songs in two tabs never overwrite each other (merge-on-write from 08-02), the same song is last-write-wins, and a song deleted elsewhere hands over to its neighbour.

Purpose: CONTEXT A5 / F4 — Andy often keeps more than one tab open; the single lh:songs key would otherwise let one tab silently revert the other.
Output: storage-event handling in js/songs.js, shared openTwoTabs helper, songs-crosstab specs (R5 first).
Ordering: runs after 08-03 (depends_on) because both plans may touch the js/songs.js flush path; the merge-write itself comes from 08-02.

Decisions implemented: A5, F4, UI-SPEC §7 (other-tab toasts), D-16 (name cut in toasts).
</objective>
