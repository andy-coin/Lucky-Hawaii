## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story form — surfaced, not invented): 歌詞改以資料模型為準，打字（含注音組字）不再被打斷；可以像 Google Docs 一樣用側邊分頁管理多首歌，並匯出、備份、換裝置還原。

<objective>
Export one song (EDIT-08): ⬇ 匯出 in the lyrics header opens a small menu — TXT (default), Markdown, PDF through the browser's print dialog, and 複製整首. Everything is built from the model, never the screen; blank sections are skipped; nothing leaves the device.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 3 (一首歌可匯出 TXT（預設）、PDF、Markdown，也可一鍵複製整首), checked on the output content.
Output: js/song-io.js, css/print.css, export menu and print sheet markup, safer fallbackCopy, songs-export specs, refreshed darwin screenshots.

Decisions implemented: CONTEXT D1, D2, D3, D4, D5, F6, F10 (js/song-io.js, css/print.css), UI-SPEC D-10, D-11, D-12 (as refined by F6), D-16 durations, D-19.

</objective>
