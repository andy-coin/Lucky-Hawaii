<assumption_delta_decision>
Signal: pluralization on "fallback" (ROADMAP goal "由 Worker 負責模型 fallback").
Singular assumption in today's code: the browser picks exactly one model from its own hard-coded list and talks to one provider branch of the Worker (`_provider` routing).
Decision: PROMOTE, not add-alongside. The plural (an ordered, dated chain of models) becomes the single source of truth and lives only in the Worker (`worker/src/models.js` + chain in `worker/src/ai.js`). The client keeps exactly one value — a preference id or 自動 — and never iterates models (`aiTryList()` returns one entry). Keeping a second, client-side fallback alongside the Worker chain would double every failed attempt against the free quota and let two lists drift apart, which is the bug class this phase removes.
</assumption_delta_decision>
