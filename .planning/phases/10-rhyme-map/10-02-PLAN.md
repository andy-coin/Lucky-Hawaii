## Phase Goal

Purpose: ROADMAP success criterion 1's "same algorithm, same results" (MAP-01), and CONTEXT A5's "repaint when data changes".
Output: tests/rhyme-parity.spec.mjs, equivalence edits in js/candidates.js and js/wordlist.js, one test in tests/lexicon-packs.spec.mjs.

Decisions implemented: CONTEXT A1 (one primitive, both sides), A5 (lexicon change). RESEARCH Open Questions used: OQ1 (空韻 grouping follows the existing filter; excluded from the three-way parity because validateWordRhyme rejects family-less requests by its AI-only guard), OQ2 (ㄣ↔ㄥ exception), OQ7 — all RESOLVED; OQ1 and OQ2 go on Andy's UAT list in 10-08.
</objective>
