## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story form — surfaced, not invented): 寫詞時一眼看出押韻結構：句尾依韻部自動上色、段落韻式統計、可選的句中押韻偵測、點字即查韻——判定一律沿用 Andy 既有的核心押韻演算法。

<objective>
Show each section's rhyme scheme as letters (AABB, ABAB…) in its header when the writer turns it on, and open a plain-text statistics popover from it: counts per rhyme, the rhyming share, and one row per line that looks up that line's tail.

Purpose: ROADMAP success criterion 2 (MAP-04) and the 「點結構字母查該行」 path of MAP-05.
Output: the 段落結構字母 switch, button.section-rhyme, #rhymeStats, lhRhymeSummary, 標題字母 / 統計 specs.

Decisions implemented: CONTEXT C1, C2, C3 (default off), C4, D2. RESEARCH OQ9 RESOLVED (one strip button per header, per-line rows inside a popover). UI-SPEC D-23 (the structure string is the popover's focal point), D-09, D-10 and D-11 (accepted overrides: rows inside the popover instead of tapping single letters; no hover popover, hover shows the native title), section 4, copy section 7. Not offered in this phase: letters drawn at the end of each line (CONTEXT C3 「也可」) — position options belong to Phase 11 MAP-11 (標記位置); listed for UAT.
</objective>
