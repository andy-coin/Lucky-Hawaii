## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story form — surfaced, not invented): 寫詞時一眼看出押韻結構：句尾依韻部自動上色、段落韻式統計、可選的句中押韻偵測、點字即查韻——判定一律沿用 Andy 既有的核心押韻演算法。

<objective>
Let the writer select a word in the lyrics and, from a small floating bar, look up its rhymes or ask for AI suggestions — without losing the selection, the caret or the keyboard, on desktop, tablet and phone.

Purpose: ROADMAP success criterion 4 (MAP-05). 10-06 adds the 「指定讀音」 button to the same bar; 10-07 adds the stats rows that reuse lhMapLookup.
Output: #rhymeSelBar and its watcher, lhMapLookup, 浮動列 / AI 建議 specs.

Decisions implemented: CONTEXT D1, D3, G1. UI-SPEC D-15, D-16, D-17, section 5. RESEARCH Pitfalls 8, 9, 10 and OQ6 (RESOLVED: query the last character; UAT item). Planner note: the lookup does exactly what the search box's input handler does (three calls), so no second lookup implementation exists.
</objective>
