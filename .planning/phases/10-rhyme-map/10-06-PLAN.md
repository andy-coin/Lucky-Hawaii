## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story form — surfaced, not invented): 寫詞時一眼看出押韻結構：句尾依韻部自動上色、段落韻式統計、可選的句中押韻偵測、點字即查韻——判定一律沿用 Andy 既有的核心押韻演算法。

<objective>
Let the writer fix the reading of a polyphonic tail character (行, 樂, 長 …) straight from the selection bar, see the color follow at once, and keep that choice through reloads, other tabs and backups — without it ever leaking onto a different line.

Purpose: MAP-08 and the second half of ROADMAP success criterion 1.
Output: 「指定讀音」 in #rhymeSelBar, the reading row, readings persistence, the import whitelist, 讀音 / 備份 specs.

Decisions implemented: CONTEXT A2 (manual reading first), A3 (per-song readings keyed by the line's last ≤ 4 characters; invalid when the key no longer matches; validated on import as a legal dictionary reading). UI-SPEC D-08 (narrowed, see truths), D-19, section 5 reading row, copy 「指定讀音」 (orchestrator copy flag). RESEARCH OQ8 and OQ12 (RESOLVED). Recorded for UAT in 10-08: A3 「手動讀音綁定句尾 4 字」, the narrower button condition, readings not being an undo step.
</objective>
