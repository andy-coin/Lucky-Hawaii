## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story form — surfaced, not invented): 寫詞時一眼看出押韻結構：句尾依韻部自動上色、段落韻式統計、可選的句中押韻偵測、點字即查韻——判定一律沿用 Andy 既有的核心押韻演算法。

<objective>
Give the writer the 「韻腳 ▾」 menu: turn tail coloring and inner-rhyme underlines on and off, switch 寬韻 / 嚴格, skip or keep tail particles, see the color legend — each applies instantly to every section and survives reloads, backups and other tabs.

Purpose: ROADMAP success criterion 3 (MAP-06, MAP-07, MAP-03); the strict mode reuses the same lhRhymeMatch / lhRhymeKey primitive (MAP-01).
Output: the toolbar button and menu, lh:map persistence, inner-rhyme detection and painting, refreshed -darwin lyrics screenshots.

Decisions implemented: CONTEXT B2 (strict keeps family colors), B4, E1, E2, E3, G1. UI-SPEC D-07, D-12, D-13, D-14, D-18, D-23 (the 開 pills are the menu's focal point), section 3 and copy (section 7). The 段落結構字母 switch row is added by 10-07 together with the header strip it controls. Planner choices recorded for UAT in 10-08: the legend has a 17th cell 「空韻」 for the neutral color; inner rhyme ignores particles while 忽略句尾語助詞 is on; lines without a tail get no underline.
</objective>
