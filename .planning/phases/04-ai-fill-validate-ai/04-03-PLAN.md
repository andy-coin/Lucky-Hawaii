## Phase Goal

**As a** 作詞人 Andy, **I want to** 按下 AI 建議時拿到的數量一定夠、造出的詞一定押韻（候選夠就由程式填滿，不夠才造詞且只造一輪，詞庫還在載入時也不會誤造詞）, **so that** 我能快速拿到真的寫得進歌詞的押韻詞。

<objective>
Make AI word generation one round, judged, and honest: one AI call per press, every word checked with its own readings before it can appear, AI badge on all of them, and a prompt that asks for exactly what the judge accepts.

Purpose: AI-04 (generation half). Today generation keeps whatever passes a broken check and the prompt for 押字首 asks for the wrong thing; after this plan generated words always rhyme by the existing rule.
Output: `generatedWordOk`, `buildGenPrompt` in js/candidates.js; rewritten `generateAIWords` in js/ai-generate.js; tests.

Decisions (IDs as defined in 04-01-PLAN.md objective): D-05 (one round per press inside generateAIWords; the caller-side flag goes in 04-04), D-06 (own readings, any polyphonic reading, unknown char → discard), D-08 (AI badge, discards only logged), D-09 (still short → show what we have, no extra call), D-13 (pure functions in Node), OD-2 (押字首 prompt = first-char rhyme family), OD-3/A3 (selected-char mode judged by the fixed-position char ∈ selectedChars, mirroring filterWordsBySelectedChars), OD-7. Claude's discretion used: request count `Math.min(remaining * 2 + 4, 40)` (judging drops words and there is only one round).
</objective>
