## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story form — surfaced, not invented): 逐行看出字數與斷句結構、對照同類段落，並在設定頁自訂所有標記；預設只開句尾上色，不讓畫面眼花撩亂。

<objective>
The writer recolours any of the 16 rhyme families from the settings, sees the change live while dragging, keeps it across themes, reloads, backups and tabs, can undo it all with a two-press reset, is warned when a colour makes lyrics hard to read — and sees every setting's effect in a live preview without leaving the overlay.

Purpose: ROADMAP success criterion 3 (adjust colour), MAP-11; UI-SPEC's preview so the settings page is not guesswork.
Output: the colour block, effective palette, reset, advisory with pure contrast helpers, the preview block and its parity test.

Decisions implemented: CONTEXT D3 (colours shared day / night, three strengths already in 11-03), D5 (colors in lh:map). UI-SPEC D-11 (legend shows effective colours), D-13, D-14 (advisory part), D-15, D-23 (預覽 is the section's focal point), section 9 (colour drag). RESEARCH Pattern 7 (preview through the shared renderer), Pattern 8 (input → variable, change → commit), Pitfall 11 (no parsing of color-mix computed values).

Override of CONTEXT D3 (recorded for UAT in 11-08): CONTEXT D3 says the 色票對照 cells themselves become native colour inputs; this plan follows UI-SPEC D-15 instead — the colour inputs live in a separate 「韻族顏色」 grid in the prefs section, and the 「韻腳 ▾」 menu legend stays plain swatches showing the effective colours (UI-SPEC D-11).

Planner choice (recorded for UAT in 11-07): the preview is repainted from lhMapMenuSync (the single "settings changed" hook already called by the setter, the storage listener, the ready gate and the lexicon change), so it can never drift from the other surfaces.
</objective>
