## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story form — surfaced, not invented): 逐行看出字數與斷句結構、對照同類段落，並在設定頁自訂所有標記；預設只開句尾上色，不讓畫面眼花撩亂。

<objective>
The writer selects some lines (or a whole verse) and presses 「📏 分析」 on the selection bar: a panel lists each touched line's count and structure next to the same line of the comparison section with ✓／≠ — on any device, with or without resident markers, never stealing the selection.

Purpose: ROADMAP success criterion 2 (the 「分析」 button trigger; touch uses the button) and criterion 1's line-by-line comparison view, MAP-09, MAP-10.
Output: the third bar button, the D-07 bar rule (and the rewritten Phase 10 assertions it overturns), the analysis panel with its states and dismissal / focus rules, and the stats rebuild fix.

Decisions implemented: CONTEXT B4, C3 (as overridden below), C5 (as overridden by UI-SPEC D-08). UI-SPEC D-07, D-08, D-09 (as corrected by RESEARCH X4), D-18, D-23 (panel focal points: title and verdicts), D-24, section 4. RESEARCH X6, Pattern 5, IN-03.

Override of CONTEXT C3 declared (orchestrator decision during plan revision; disclosed in the 11-08 UAT list): C3 locks 「有對照段落時多一欄」 — an extra column for the comparison line. This plan keeps UI-SPEC D-09's layout instead: the comparison appears as a full-width row under each line (「對照 {名} 第 {i} 行：{n} 字　{結構}」 + ✓／≠), because the panel is min(24rem, 100vw − 1rem) wide and at 390px a fifth column cannot fit next to 行 / 文字 / 字數 / 結構 without cutting the lyric text to a few characters. The content C3 asks for (the comparison line's count and structure plus ✓／≠ for every selected line) is unchanged; only its position moves from a column to a row.

Structure cells follow 11-01's guessed-run rule (override of UI-SPEC D-04): the panel renders lhLineStructParts, so on a line like 月光 灑在 窗前 夢裡想起你的臉 only 「≈2+2+3」 is slanted inside 「2+2+2+≈2+2+3」.
</objective>
