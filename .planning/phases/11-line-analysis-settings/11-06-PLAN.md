## Phase Goal

<objective>
A writer who prefers it turns on 「反白停留自動分析」: after selecting lines with the mouse and resting for 1.5 seconds, the 句構 panel opens by itself; touch devices keep using the 「📏 分析」 button, and the feature is off for everyone until chosen.

Purpose: ROADMAP success criterion 2 (hover after selection; touch uses the button), MAP-10; its switch completes MAP-11's list.
Output: the autoHover switch row and the dwell detector with page.clock tests.

Decisions implemented: CONTEXT C4, C5, D2, D4. UI-SPEC D-10 (zone = textarea width × selected visual rows, measured once), section 7 hint copy. RESEARCH Pattern 6 (mouse pointerType check on top of the media query for iPadOS trackpads, Assumption A2).
</objective>
