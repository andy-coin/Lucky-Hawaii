## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story form — surfaced, not invented): 逐行看出字數與斷句結構、對照同類段落，並在設定頁自訂所有標記；預設只開句尾上色，不讓畫面眼花撩亂。

<objective>
The writer picks where the markers sit (靠右欄 or 句尾後), how big they are (小／中／大) and how strong the tail colouring is (淡／標準／濃) in 「顯示方式」, sees the change instantly without losing the caret, selection or scroll — and the lyrics never re-wrap because of a marker, at any width, zoom or scrollbar.

Purpose: ROADMAP success criterion 3 (adjust marker position, text size, colour strength), MAP-11; the CONTEXT E1 alignment guarantee behind MAP-09's markers.
Output: the 顯示方式 radios, tail-mode CSS, the depth table and band variables, lm-typing for tail mode, contrast unit tests, and the wrap-identity / last-visual-row / staleness / composition held-out tests.

Decisions implemented: CONTEXT C2, C5, D3 (position, size, strength), E1, E2. UI-SPEC D-01, D-02 (as corrected by RESEARCH X2), D-03, D-06, D-14, D-21, D-22, section 2.2, section 9 (position switch, zoom). RESEARCH X1, X2, Pattern 8, Pitfall 1, 2, 8.

Planner choice (recorded for UAT in 11-07): in 句尾後 the diff token joins the count with a plain space (「7 ≠8」) while the other tokens are joined by 「 · 」, matching the UI-SPEC examples for both positions.
</objective>
