## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story form — surfaced, not invented): 逐行看出字數與斷句結構、對照同類段落，並在設定頁自訂所有標記；預設只開句尾上色，不讓畫面眼花撩亂。

<objective>
The writer opens 「更多設定…」 and gets the full 「韻腳地圖與句構分析」 section in ⚙️ 偏好設定: every Phase 10 rhyme switch plus 逐行標記 and its four content items. Turning on 對照差異 makes Verse II and Verse III flag every line whose count differs from Verse I; 行尾韻腳字母 adds the rhyme letter; guessed structures fill in by themselves once the word list arrives — and a long song with all of it on still types smoothly.

Purpose: ROADMAP success criteria 1 (Verse I vs Verse II line by line) and 3 (switch each rhyme-map and line-analysis item on its own), MAP-09, MAP-11.
Output: the prefs section (switches part), 「更多設定…」, two-surface sync, comparison and letter tokens, same-type scheduling, Esc for the overlay, a gated perf measurement.

Decisions implemented: CONTEXT A4, B1, B2 (no manual picker), B3, C1, D1, D2 (the 反白停留自動分析 row arrives with its behaviour in 11-06), E2. UI-SPEC D-04, D-09 (as corrected by RESEARCH X4), D-11, D-12, D-17, D-18, D-19, D-23 (the 開 pills stay the section's quiet focal points until 11-04 adds the preview). RESEARCH X4, X7, X8, X9, Pitfall 5, Pitfall 7, Pitfall 10.

Planner choices (recorded for UAT in 11-07): because RESEARCH X4 fixes the comparison to the first same-type section, the 對照差異 hint says 「跟同類型第一段的同一行字數不同時，標出 ≠ 和對方的字數」 instead of the UI-SPEC wording 「同類型前一段」 (the copy must not describe a different rule than the one implemented); the overlay gets Esc (X7) but no focus return, because the ⚙️ trigger is a non-focusable div today.
</objective>
